Methodology and limits

Open Future Forum research draws on first-party surveys, qualitative interviews, executive discussions and other data collected from its executive network. Individual reports contain their own methodology notes.

Raw survey and respondent-level data is not published. Repository summaries provide context and source paths; they do not replace the canonical report or turn a cited claim into a universal market conclusion.
