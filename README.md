## Scope and methodology

Open Future Forum research uses first-party survey, application, interview, and discussion material from its network. Each canonical report states its response base, limitations, and edition-specific methodology. Raw respondent-level data is not published in this repository.

The repository records are descriptive. They do not rank the reports, make claims about their independence from the publisher, or imply endorsement by cited third parties.
